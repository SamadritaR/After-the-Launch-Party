The reading. Company conviction is almost flat across the whole set (average 8.9 out of 10), so it predicts nothing about survival. The two signals that actually separate winners from the faded are real usage and whether competitors copied the feature, both signals the company does not control. Pricing tied to a customer outcome outscored every other model, and being an AI native startup proved to be no moat over established software companies.
